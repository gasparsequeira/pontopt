i'm diogo gaspar sequeira. this is my personal blog, where i host my writings.

for convenience's sake, i also host hyperlinks to legislation that i frequently need to consult.

you can reach me at diogogasparsequeira@gmail.com.

i used to design websites in high school (i studied visual arts before law school). my father used to say, "well, at least you'll have o site mais bonito out of all the lawyers." not quite sure about that.

this website is hand-coded from scratch using Visual Studio Code on my Windows machine, Cursor solely as an IDE (no paid plan) on my Macbook Air ( Sublime for quick fixes) and hosted on Github. it will never feature third-party ads, affiliate links, cookies, or art/text/code made by generative ai. it's a simple blog that i've written as an exercise in what i learned in high school - and in writing things down / sharing with others / having fun online?
It’s not meant to be a “professional portfolio” or an online CV. It’s more of a personal blog, really! one that happens to be useful to me, and hopefully to you too!

im also quite active on X, if that's your cup of tea*.

* Views expressed are my own and do not represent those of any client, firm, or organization I am affiliated with.
